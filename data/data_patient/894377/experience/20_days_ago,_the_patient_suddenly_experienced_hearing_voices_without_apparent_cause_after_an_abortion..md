One day after my miscarriage, I suddenly started hearing voices that seemed to come from the air, following me wherever I went. They were not any familiar voices, but inexplicable whispers and noises, as if someone was whispering in my ear, but when I turned around, there was no one there. These voices made me extremely confused and frightened, because I clearly knew there was no one else in the room, but the voices were so real that I could not ignore them.
